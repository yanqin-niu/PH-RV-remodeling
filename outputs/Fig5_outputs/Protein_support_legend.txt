Bubble color and size show transcriptomic direction and absolute log2 fold change. Shapes annotate significant direction-consistent protein evidence, not independent-subject validation. Tissue: imported dRV-versus-CTRL log2Ratio, qval_Limma<0.05 and |zscore|>=2. Olink: supplied adjusted P<0.05 in PAH-versus-CTRL or decompensated-PAH-versus-CTRL, oriented as disease-minus-control NPX. No protein twofold cutoff is imposed. Significant opposite assays within a contrast prevent a support call. The Olink shape is the union of two related comparisons; their results are separately exported. Reference direction is discovery RV for Figure 5 and RV-wide consensus for Figure S5. Transcript only means no qualifying directional protein evidence, including unmeasured/unmapped genes, not a negative protein test. Frozen one-to-one orthology and exact human symbols are used; ambiguous tissue protein groups are excluded. Protein evidence does not enter transcriptomic selection. Participant overlap with transcriptomic cohorts cannot be excluded from available files. See Protein_Gene_assay, Protein_Gene_contrast, Protein_Summary and Protein_Methods in source data.
