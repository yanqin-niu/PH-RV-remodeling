Figure S8. Technical-stratum sensitivity of GSE240921. (A) VST PCA fitted separately by stratum; no sample exclusions. (B) Sex-adjusted dRV-versus-Ctrl log2FC estimates in SE15 and PE25. Red points mark the fixed35-panel human orthologues. (C) Strict DEG counts for group-only, sex-adjusted separate fits and the joint Batch+Sex+Group fit. (D) Discovery-direction-aligned effects for the fixed35, with stars denoting FDR<0.05, |log2FC|>=1 and expected direction; grey denotes unavailable effects. The primary reference is the same fixed panel in all columns. The interaction analysis in source data tests (dRV-Ctrl)SE15 minus (dRV-Ctrl)PE25 using all40 samples with sex adjustment and transcriptome-wide FDR. Nonsignificance is not equivalence. Technical strata may also differ in donor/collection backgrounds and are not treated as independent study-level votes.
