(A) Direction-aligned LV-only, RV-only and Shared scores in GSE240923 batch1 across Ctrl, cRV, early_dRV and late_dRV. (B) Corresponding scores in batch2 across Ctrl, cRV and dRV. Raw counts were summed by symbol, normalized separately within batch using DESeq2 poscounts size factors, transformed as log2(normalized counts+1), and standardized per gene within batch. Each gene Z score was multiplied by its discovery sign before averaging (LV discovery effect for LV-only; RV effect for RV-only and Shared). Missing or constant genes were excluded; Shared coverage was 90/108 in both batches, comprising 79 upregulated and 11 downregulated reference genes. Boxes and individual points show raw aligned scores. Group P values are HC3-robust Wald F tests from categorical-group models, additionally adjusted for Sex in batch2. Control-versus-stage brackets use HC3-robust contrasts with Holm correction over all nine contrasts in batch1 or six in batch2 (* adjusted P<0.05, **<0.01, ***<0.001). These annotations do not compare stage-tracking performance between signatures. Ordinal trends, within-sample paired signature comparisons, sex effects and pointwise 95% intervals are exported with up/down components, coverage and size factors.
